luach (Hebrew for "board") is a private family calendar. Instead of opening a calendar app, the people in the household tell luach what is happening — "dentist next Tuesday at three" — by typing, by voice, or by forwarding an email, and luach puts it on the one shared calendar.

Sign in Privacy policy

What it does

  • Keeps one shared calendar for the household, in Google Calendar, with an agenda, a touch board for the kitchen and a list of tasks and chores.
  • Turns words into events. An assistant reads what someone said and proposes the event. Anything it is unsure of — a vague time, something in the past, a possible duplicate, a change to someone else's event — waits for a person to approve it.
  • Reminds people before things start, the evening before, and once a week.
  • Lets people book a meeting with a member through a booking link that only offers times when the household is free.
  • Optionally reads a member's own Gmail, read-only. A member who connects their Gmail can ask the assistant about their mail ("when is our flight?"), and can turn on a scan that reads new mail and proposes appointments it finds — a dentist's reminder, a flight, a school trip. Nothing is added until that member says yes, and no one else in the household can see or search their mail.
